Determination of eyebrow starting points

1min 10s

Using a pencil and a surgical ruler, the first step is to locate the exact center of the face, between the two eyes. A vertical line is drawn to mark this center. Then, using the ruler vertically, parallel lines are drawn to mark the starting point of each eyebrow, typically aligned with the bridge of the nose.
